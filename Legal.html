1. Terms and Conditions

1.1 About the service

The app lets registered users browse and order export-grade butterfly pupae from breeders, and book visits to participating butterfly farms. It also includes a photo-based identification tool. Jerwin M. Montellano / LepVision provides the app as a platform. Breeders and farm owners are responsible for the products and services they offer.

1.2 Eligibility and accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old, or have a parent or guardian's consent, to create an account or place an order.
  • You must give accurate information, including your name, phone number, email, address, and, for buyers, a valid government ID type and number.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password secret and for everything done under your account. Tell us at once if you suspect unauthorised use.
  • Farm owner and administrator accounts are created by Jerwin M. Montellano / LepVision only. New accounts may be issued a temporary password, which you must change at first login.

1.3 Buying pupae

  • Listings show the price set by each breeder. Prices are in Philippine pesos (₱) and include any stated fees.
  • A fresh pupae listing stays on sale for 2 days after it is posted. After that, or when stock runs out, it cannot be bought.
  • Placing an order reserves stock. An order stays Awaiting Payment until you pay and submit a GCash reference number. Unpaid orders may be cancelled by you or the seller.
  • Payment is made directly to the seller's GCash account. Jerwin M. Montellano / LepVision does not hold or process payments, and no escrow applies unless we say so in writing.
  • Breeders are responsible for the health, quality, and legal status of the pupae they sell, including any permits required for export or transport of wildlife. Buyers must comply with the laws of the Philippines and of their destination country, including CITES and the Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act (RA 9147, as amended).
  • Refunds and replacements for defective or dead pupae are handled between buyer and seller. Please report problems promptly through the app's messages.

1.4 Farm visits

  • A visit request is Awaiting Approval until the farm owner approves it. Approval does not guarantee attendance if the farm closes or the slot becomes unavailable; the farm will tell you and offer another date.
  • Visit fees are paid via GCash to the farm. Cancellation and rescheduling rules are set by each farm and shown in the app.
  • Visitors must follow farm safety rules, stay in designated areas, and not handle or remove insects or plants without staff permission. Farm owners may refuse entry or remove a visitor who breaks these rules, without refund where the rules are broken.
  • Children under 18 must be accompanied by an adult who accepts these terms on their behalf.

1.5 Photos and the identification tool

  • Photos you upload are used to identify the butterfly and to build a research dataset. Upload only photos you have the right to share. Do not upload photos of people or sensitive personal information.

2. Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its implementing rules.

  • Account details: username, full name, phone number, email, address, organization or farm name, GCash number (for sellers and farm owners).

  • Transactions: orders, visit bookings, GCash reference numbers you submit, amounts, dates, and messages between users about bookings.

  • To create and manage your account, process orders and bookings, and send related notices.
